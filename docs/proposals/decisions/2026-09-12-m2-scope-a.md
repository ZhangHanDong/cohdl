# M2 scope decision: Candidate A

Date: 2026-09-12. Status: **user-selected proposal scope, not formal RFC acceptance**.

After the assistant recommended A first and explained its difference from B, the user replied “好，按你的建议继续”. This record selects A for the next M2 revision. The formal RFC number remains unassigned; the Accepted language specification has not changed.

## Selected boundary

M2 proposes bounded Int/Length evaluation for counts, indexes and placement, shared fn/subdesign integer parameters, physical/subdesign array selectors, and finite loops over admitted operations. Loops may repeat wiring, nc and effectful fn calls; legal layout bodies may repeat placement. Existing fn calls can still create ordinary local components.

Direct scalar/array inst and subdesign declarations inside loop bodies are **deferred with Candidate B**, including in empty loops. A uses ordinary circuit-body declarations, named arrays and existing fn/subdesign composition. It does not make fn pure or give fn locals an external placement interface. A loop label names an operation site, not an electrically addressable or placeable group.

The proposed shared declaration validator, full expansion-graph budgets, deterministic provenance and check/build/fmt/LSP/docs/Explorer contracts remain in A. They are proposed obligations, not implementation claims.

## Why A

The existing-syntax [RC workflow](../fixtures/m2-programmability/rc-workflow/README.md) already expresses ten named channels, a top-level override of the seventh capacitor, and growth to twelve channels. A aims to remove repetitive quantities, connections and placements while preserving those named subdesign paths. The LED chain and nested reuse scenarios also fit that boundary.

This is a scope judgment supported by the existing-syntax baseline and the [source comparison](../../reviews/2026-09-11-tscircuit-rfc-review.md), not a benchmark of implemented A/B variants. The cost is that ad hoc repeated private component construction requires an existing fn or subdesign definition. That extraction cost is accepted for this proposal scope; a future B proposal needs a concrete workload that justifies its extra declaration and identity rules.

## Still open

- Whether loop identity labels are always required; the draft's labelled spelling remains a comparison baseline.
- Whether to add half-open fan-out alongside existing inclusive fan-out; selecting A does not decide this spelling.
- Acceptance of the stated lexical compatibility tradeoffs, including minus parsing and the existing tolerance `%` suffix versus integer remainder.
- The proposed uniform declaration-check correction, contingent on a measured whole-library before/after audit, then formal governance updates.
